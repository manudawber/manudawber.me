What the benchmark does

  • 0–20%: Spirals outward from center to screen edges (tests blur ramp-up).
  • 20–75%: Traces the outer viewport perimeter & corners (peak blur + peak scale).
  • 75–100%: Fast diagonal corner-to-corner sweeps.
  • Theme toggles: Clicks #theme-toggle mid-motion at 20%, 45%, 70%, and 88% (Light → Dark → Light → Dark → Light).

How to read the numbers

  • 1% Low FPS: Effective FPS of the worst 1% of frames (best indicator of perceived stutter).
  • Theme Spike: Worst single-frame latency within ±3 frames of a Light/Dark switch (catches texture re-baking stalls).
  • Dropped %: Frames exceeding 1.5× your display's refresh interval.
  • Timeline: Green ≤ budget, amber ≤ 2× budget, red > 2× budget; blue ticks mark theme toggles.
